# VeilAid Level 2 Demo Script

Target length: **1 minute 35 seconds**. Keep the credential file closed for the
entire recording.

| Time | Screen action | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Open the live VeilAid URL. | “VeilAid lets aid recipients prove eligibility without publishing their identity or eligibility evidence.” |
| 0:10–0:25 | Click **Connect Lace**, approve, show the address and Preprod badge. | “The DApp connects through Midnight’s current Connector API and verifies that Lace is on Preprod.” |
| 0:25–0:38 | Point to the active campaign and contract address. | “This public Preprod contract stores the eligibility commitment and auditable claim count.” |
| 0:38–0:48 | Click **Select credential** and choose the private file. | “The credential is read only into this browser session. Its secret never appears in the interface, URL, or logs.” |
| 0:48–1:12 | Click **Prove & claim aid**; show progress. | “The private witness runs locally, Lace generates the zero-knowledge proof, balances it, and submits only the proof result.” |
| 1:12–1:26 | Show transaction reference, block, and count. | “The chain confirms one valid claim and exposes a receipt for accountability.” |
| 1:26–1:35 | Point to the Privacy Claim panel. | “An observer sees the claim result, but cannot see the recipient, the secret, or why they qualify.” |

Before recording:

- Use a clean browser window at 100% zoom.
- Unlock Lace and select Preprod.
- Confirm the Lace wallet has NIGHT/DUST.
- Confirm the production contract has not already accepted this credential.
- Keep DevTools closed so no unrelated wallet messages distract from the demo.
- Record at 1080p and make the pointer movements slow and deliberate.
